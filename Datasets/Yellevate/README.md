
# Yellevate – Invoice Dispute Analysis

## 📌 Overview
This project analyzes invoice disputes for Yellevate to identify operational bottlenecks and quantify revenue loss.

Goal: Determine the root cause of dispute-driven **5% annual revenue loss** and propose process improvements.

---
## 🧰 Tools Used
- **Excel** (Charts, Pivot Tables, Dashboard)
- **SQL** (Data cleaning & preprocessing)

---
## 🔍 Problem Statement
Yellevate lacked a clear dispute resolution process and consistent client onboarding–offboarding documentation.
This caused recurring disputes and revenue loss.

---
## 🗂 Your Actions
- Cleaned billing, disputes, and client workflow datasets
- Built pivot-based analysis and Excel dashboards
- Conducted root-cause analysis
- Created stakeholder-friendly visuals

---
## 📊 Results & Insights
- Identified exact drivers of **~5% annual revenue loss**
- Highlighted inconsistencies in client scope documentation
- Recommended unified onboarding/offboarding contract terms
- Suggested dispute workflow improvements
